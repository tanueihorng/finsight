A1 · Launch

Two ways to start: demo or your real book.

Demo opens a separate FinSight with a made-up portfolio, so you can present without showing your holdings. Real runs on your own data folder.

  1. Demo: run npm run demo or double-click start-demo.command. Opens 127.0.0.1:8003, PIN 2468.
  2. Real: run node server.ts (or start.command) and open localhost:8000.
  3. Kestrel starts with FinSight automatically once npm run agent:install has been run and a model key is set.
Terminal · demo
npm run demo
Reset the demo book any time with npm run demo:reset. Needs Node 24+.

A2 · Opening Bell

Pick the book, then unlock.

The lock screen is called the Opening Bell. Before typing your PIN, choose Real portfolio or Paper trading. The choice sticks across tabs and Kestrel sees the same book you see.

  1. Click REAL PORTFOLIO or PAPER TRADING.
  2. Type your 4-digit PIN and press UNLOCK → (or Enter).
  3. The 🔒 button in the top bar locks it again.
Forgot the PIN? Delete auth.json in your data folder. Holdings are kept.

B3 · Positions

B7 · FX risk

Fund in SGD, invest in USD? Watch this panel.

Every buy records the exchange rate at that moment, so FinSight knows your blended entry rate and how much the currency alone has made or cost you.

  1. What-if slider: drag a USD/SGD shock to see the impact on your book.
  2. 1-week VaR: a typical weekly swing and the 95% bad case.
  3. Target exposure and Convert now vs later help plan the next SGD→USD conversion.
Buys from the last ~7 days show near-zero FX P&L ("settling"). That's by design.

D1 · The pattern

Three lenses, one challenge round, one chair.

Each specialist gives a verdict with evidence for and against, an uncertainty level and what would change their mind. The chair weighs evidence, not headcount.

  1. Bounded: a fixed research budget per run, cost shown when done.
  2. Never automatic, never trades. Figures still come only from tools.
  3. If a company is ambiguous or data is missing, it says so instead of guessing.

E1 · Get set up

Flip to PAPER, connect once.

Choose Paper trading on the lock screen, or click PAPER in the top bar. The amber stripe and PAPER badge tell you which book you're in.

E3 · Your first order

Eight clicks from idea to order.

  1. Pick BUY or SELL and type the symbol.
  2. Market fills now; Limit waits for your price.
  3. Size in Shares (fractions OK) or US$, or tap 25/50/75/100%.
  4. Time in force Day or GTC, check the estimate, press Buy.
  5. The fill streams in: positions, ledger and P&L update without refreshing.
In Positions, Close pre-fills a full sell; you still press Sell to send it.

E4 · Agent orders

Kestrel can trade paper. You set the leash.

In PAPER mode Kestrel gets three tools: read the paper account, place an order, cancel an order. What happens next is your Settings choice.

  1. Ask me first (default): a highlighted ticket waits on the desk for Approve or Reject.
  2. Autonomous: orders go straight to Alpaca paper.
  3. Limits refuse an order before it reaches Alpaca, with the reason: max order, per-day count, max position %, symbol allowlist.
Only you can change these settings, from the terminal behind your PIN. Every order is tagged user or kestrel.

F2 · Built to be trusted

What it will and won't do.

LocalRuns on your machine. No sign-up, no telemetry. Data lives in your data folder.
LockedHashed PIN with throttling. Kestrel runs on loopback behind it.
Real bookKestrel is read-only: it can't trade, move money, message anyone or edit records. Facts become proposals.
Paper bookKestrel can place Alpaca paper orders only, with your approval or within your limits. No live-trading URL exists in the code.
NumbersTotals, weights and sizes come from tools, never model arithmetic. Answers cite asOf and sources.
SpendPer-answer and daily caps; /limits to change.
